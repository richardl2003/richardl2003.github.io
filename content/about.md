## Principles

An evolving list of principles I try to live by. Most of these came from experience, conversations, or things I've read that stuck with me.

### Do what you say

Being reliable is following through on the little things that seem forgettable. Trust compounds in both directions: keeping your word builds it slowly, and breaking it undoes that work fast. I want to be someone people can count on, not just for the important things, but for all of them.
